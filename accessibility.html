The person comes first

Accessibility

There is one Inner Thought—not an accessible edition and a default edition. Access is part of whether the promise is true.

◇

Our standard: a feature is not complete when its controls have labels. It is complete when the whole task can be understood and finished using the access method a person relies on.

Designed around different ways of interacting

Inner Thought is built to support VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, hardware keyboards, Larger Text, Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, Reduce Motion, and light and dark appearances.

Recording, playback, saving, errors, and location state are never communicated through colour, sound, motion, or haptics alone. Gestures are accelerators rather than the only available path.

Words remain the content

Text uses Dynamic Type and layouts are designed to grow rather than hide essential actions. Spoken controls use localized names and values. Live transcription is optional, and a voice recording remains safe when speech recognition is unavailable.

Photos and videos include a place for a person-supplied description. Playback exposes elapsed and total time plus an adjustable progress control.

Movement, contrast, and attention

Reduce Motion provides a composed static experience. Important state uses text and shape as well as colour, and the interface responds to stronger contrast and colour-differentiation settings. Atmospheric movement pauses when it is not useful.

How we qualify a release

Automated checks cover enlarged text, deliberately expanded copy, Arabic right-to-left layout, every supported interface language, and primary navigation on iPhone and iPad. Physical-device testing then covers complete tasks with assistive technologies, recording, permissions, widgets, and Live Activities.

We publish an App Store accessibility label only after every common task included in Apple’s criteria has been verified for that device family. We would rather leave a claim unpublished than overstate what has been proven.

Tell us where access breaks

An accessibility failure is a product failure. If anything stands between you and capturing, finding, reading, hearing, editing, exporting, or deleting a thought, email support@movingtruth.com. Tell us the device, iOS or iPadOS version, access feature, and task. Please do not include a private thought unless you deliberately want us to see it.
