A place to catch what would otherwise vanish.

Inner Thought exists to help people catch their creativity before it disappears—and to do so with privacy, dignity, and grace. Speak it, type it, photograph it, or film it. Organize it later, or not at all.

Ready when thought arrives. Quiet when it doesn’t.

Capture comes first

The thought does not need to be ready.

A title can wait. A category can wait. The idea cannot. Inner Thought shortens the distance between the moment something arrives and the moment it is safe.

VoiceKeep the original recording and, when you choose, its complete transcript.
TextDictate without keeping audio, or simply type what came to you.
PlaceAdd a one-time location only when that context belongs to the thought.
MediaHold photographs and video privately inside the memory.
◎ Accessibility from day one

One Inner Thought. For everyone.

Accessibility is part of whether the promise is true—not a separate edition and not a final polish pass. Every common task is being designed and tested across different ways of seeing, hearing, moving, reading, and interacting.

VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, Larger Text, Reduce Motion, contrast, keyboard access, and clear non-audio state are release requirements.

◇ Private by design

Your thoughts are not the product.

Inner Thought requires no account and contains no in-app advertising or third-party analytics. Saved titles, words, locations, custom tags, recordings, photos, and videos are encrypted on your device with a key held by iOS Keychain.

  • No background location tracking.
  • No broad photo-library crawl.
  • On-device transcription; no transcript content in widgets or logs by default.
  • No subscription beside your private thoughts.
